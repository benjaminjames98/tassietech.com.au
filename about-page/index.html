Our story

Local heart, serious technical depth.

TassieTech is built around a simple belief: technology should make work easier, safer, and more productive. We are not here to overwhelm clients with jargon. We are here to pair the right technology with the right practical support.

Our team brings experience across software development, websites, networks, cloud platforms, security, computer systems, and day-to-day support. That breadth matters because real IT problems rarely fit neatly inside one category.

What clients should feel

  • Friendly help from people who respect your time.
  • Reliable follow-through and sensible advice.
  • Professional standards without confusing language.
  • Technology choices that fit local Tasmanian businesses.
Why local matters

Local Tasmanian IT support. Not mainlanders with a local number.

We live and work here. Our clients are neighbours, local employers, community services, trades, retailers, clinics, schools, and growing Tasmanian businesses that need technology to be simpler.
