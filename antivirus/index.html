Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Do I need to pay for antivirus?

It depends on your device and how you use it. We give honest advice on what protection you actually need, without commissions or upselling.

Can I have more than one antivirus installed?

It is best not to. Two antivirus products can conflict and slow your computer down. We help you choose one and set it up properly.

Do Macs need antivirus?

Macs are targeted less often, but they can still pick up adware, scam pop-ups, and malware. We can advise on the right protection for your Mac.

My antivirus says something is wrong. What should I do?

Do not ignore it. Call us and we can check whether it is a real threat and clean it up if needed.
