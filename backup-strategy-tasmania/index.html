Backups

A backup is only useful if recovery works.

Many businesses assume sync is backup, or that a drive sitting onsite is enough. A stronger backup strategy separates collaboration from recovery, considers ransomware and accidental deletion, and tests whether important data can actually be restored.

We help with backup design, Synology NAS planning, cloud backup, Microsoft 365 data considerations, retention, restore testing, documentation, and practical recovery steps for devices, servers, websites, and business files.

What good looks like

  • Important data has a clear recovery path.
  • Backup is separated from normal file sync.
  • Restore testing becomes part of the support rhythm.
Stack fit

Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Is Dropbox or OneDrive the same as backup?

No. Sync can be useful, but it is not always a complete backup strategy. Recovery planning needs retention, restore testing, and protection from accidental or malicious changes.

Do you support Synology NAS devices?

Yes. Synology NASs are part of our stack where they fit the business need, especially for local storage, backup workflows, and continuity planning.

Can you test whether our backups work?

Yes. Restore testing is one of the most important parts of backup planning because an untested backup is only an assumption.

Can your business recover from data loss?

Tell us where your files, systems, and websites live. We will help identify the gaps between sync, backup, and real recovery.
