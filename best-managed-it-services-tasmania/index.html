Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

What is managed IT support?

Managed IT support means we look after your business technology on an ongoing basis. Instead of calling someone only when something breaks, we monitor, maintain, and support your systems so problems are caught early and your team stays productive.

How is managed IT different from break-fix IT support?

With break-fix support you call a technician after something goes wrong and pay per job. Managed IT is proactive: we keep your systems maintained and monitored to prevent problems and reduce downtime.

Is managed IT support worth it for a small business?

For most small businesses, yes. You get the benefits of an IT department without the cost of hiring in-house staff, along with more stable systems and someone who already knows your setup when you need help.

How much does managed IT support cost?

It depends on the size of your team, your devices, and the systems you use. We talk through your needs first, then give you a tailored quote.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Yes. We start by reviewing your current systems and how your business uses them, then recommend changes only where they will make a real difference.

Do you offer on-site support for businesses?

Yes. When you need someone on-site, we can be there. We are based in Latrobe and support businesses across Tasmania.
