Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Where should a small business start with cyber security?

Start with the basics that stop most attacks: MFA on every account, a password manager, managed antivirus, updates, and backups you have tested. We help you prioritise.

Do you train staff?

Yes. Security awareness training helps your team recognise phishing, scams, and risky behaviour.

What does being an ASD partner mean?

We are part of the Australian Signals Directorate cyber security partnership program and follow ASD guidance when securing networks and responding to threats.

What should we do if we think we have been hacked?

Call us straight away on 03 6449 4214. Our emergency response page explains the first steps.
