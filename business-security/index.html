Why us

Why Choose Tassie Tech for Business Security?

Commercial-Grade Protection, Local Service

In today’s digital landscape, cyber-security is an ever larger and more complex concern for businesses. Tassie Tech deploys enterprise-grade antivirus and antimalware, develops cyber-security policies, provides staff training, and hardens your systems against targeted intrusions, ransomware, and malicious insiders. We protect workstations, laptops, mobiles, servers, and networks – the same calibre of tools used by large enterprises, tailored and priced for Tasmanian businesses. We also provide security support for individuals, so our techs understand threats at every level.

ASD Partner, Tasmanian Based

Tassie Tech is an Australian Signals Directorate Business Partner, following ASD guidelines to manage secure networks and quickly combat threats. We’re based right here in Tasmania and serve businesses across the state. See all the areas we serve.

Proactive, Not Reactive

Our approach includes ongoing monitoring, regular system hardening, and staff training to reduce your attack surface before threats reach your door.

Plain-Language Security Advice

We genuinely like people, and do our best to make cyber-security approachable, not intimidating. We explain risks and recommendations clearly, so you can make informed decisions about protecting your business and your customers’ data.

We try hard to be the best IT company we can be. Give us a try; you won’t regret it!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Tassie Tech

What does Tassie Tech actually help with?

We handle a wide range of computer and IT issues including slow systems, startup problems, hardware faults, software issues, and general troubleshooting for both home and business users.

Do you only work with businesses or individuals too?

We support both. Whether it’s a personal laptop issue or ongoing business IT support, we apply the same level of expertise and care.

How experienced are your technicians?

Our team has a strong track record across all major IT areas with a solve rate of over 98%. We deal with real-world issues daily, so most problems we see are familiar and fixable quickly.

Are you based in Tasmania?

Yes. We’re a local Tasmanian team, serving areas across the North West Coast including Burnie, Devonport, Latrobe, Ulverstone, and surrounding regions.

Why not just use a big national IT company?

Many larger IT providers outsource work or rely on third-party technicians. We work directly with local techs, which keeps costs down and communication clear.

Will I understand what you’re doing to my computer?

Yes. We focus on clear, simple explanations so you always understand the issue and the solution without technical jargon.

How fast can you fix my issue?

It depends on the problem, but our focus is always on fast turnaround times and getting you back up and running as quickly as possible.

Do I need to be technical to work with you?

Not at all. We handle the technical side and guide you through everything in a straightforward way.
