Managed service plans

Support for every part of your business.

Our care packages bundle support, security, management, training, and backup into three levels: Good, Better, and Best. Every plan includes guaranteed response times, managed antivirus, unlimited remote support, and a dedicated support agent you can call directly.

Not sure which level fits? We will review your team, devices, and risks first, then recommend the plan that makes sense.

Reading the table

  • ✓ means the item is included in the plan.
  • TTS Care rates means the work is available and charged at our care rates.
  • Extra means it can be added to the plan.
  • Response times are set out in our response time guide.
