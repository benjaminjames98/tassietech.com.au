Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

What does TassieTech help with?

We handle a wide range of computer issues, including slow systems, startup problems, hardware faults, software issues, and general troubleshooting for home and business users.

Do you work with individuals as well as businesses?

Yes. Whether it is a personal laptop issue or ongoing business IT support, we apply the same level of expertise and care.

Do you work on laptops, Macs, and Windows PCs?

Yes. We support every kind of computer. Our laptop repairs, PC repairs, Windows support, and Mac support pages have the details.

Will I understand what you are doing to my computer?

Yes. We focus on clear, simple explanations so you understand the issue and the solution without technical jargon.

How fast can you fix my issue?

It depends on the problem, but we always focus on fast turnaround and getting you back up and running as quickly as possible.
