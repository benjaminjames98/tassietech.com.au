Cyber security

Security that fits the way people actually work.

Cyber security does not need to be theatrical to be effective. The biggest gains often come from strengthening accounts, patching devices, using MFA, managing passwords properly, checking backups, and making sure staff know what to do when something feels wrong.

We help with security reviews, endpoint protection, Microsoft 365 security basics, 1Password rollout, phishing and scam guidance, backup and restore planning, incident triage, and practical policies that staff can follow.

What good looks like

  • Accounts are harder to compromise.
  • Backups are easier to trust during an incident.
  • Staff know when to pause, report, and escalate.

Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Do you provide cyber security for small businesses?

Yes. Most of our security work is practical small-business protection: accounts, devices, backups, Microsoft 365, passwords, staff habits, and response planning.

Can you help after a suspected compromise?

Yes. If operations have stopped, data may be at risk, or accounts may be compromised, call first so we can triage faster.

Do you recommend password managers?

Yes. 1Password is part of our supported stack because unique passwords and shared vaults are more reliable than reused passwords or spreadsheets.

Want a practical security review?

Tell us what systems your business relies on and we will help identify the highest-value security improvements first.
