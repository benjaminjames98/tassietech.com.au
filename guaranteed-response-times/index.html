Guaranteed Response Times

Managed service response priorities should be clear before something goes wrong. This page explains the intent: urgent business disruption receives priority, while lower-impact requests are scheduled sensibly.

Priority guide

  • Critical: business-wide outage, suspected compromise, or major operational disruption.
  • High: important staff or systems blocked with no practical workaround.
  • Normal: individual support requests, minor issues, changes, and improvements.
  • Planned: projects, procurement, migrations, and scheduled maintenance.

For exact response commitments, refer to your current managed service agreement or contact TassieTech.
