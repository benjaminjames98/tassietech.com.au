Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Do you provide onsite support everywhere in Tasmania?

We are North West based. Onsite support is strongest around our local region, while many business support tasks can be handled remotely across Tasmania and onsite work can be planned where it makes sense.

Can Hobart, Launceston, and East Coast businesses still work with TassieTech?

Yes. We support businesses through remote tools, Microsoft 365 administration, cloud systems, security work, website support, and planned project delivery across Tasmania.

Which towns do your service area pages cover?

The site includes public service-area pages for Burnie, Devonport, Launceston, Hobart, Wynyard, Smithton, Penguin, Queenstown, Zeehan, George Town, Perth, Campbell Town, St Helens, New Norfolk, Sorell, Kingston, Huonville, and other Tasmanian locations.
