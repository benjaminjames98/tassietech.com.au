Laptop problems we fix

HP, Dell, Lenovo, Acer, ASUS, Surface, and more.

Part of our computer repairs service. Got a MacBook? See Mac support.

Battery and charging problems

Batteries that drain fast, will not charge, or swell, plus loose or damaged charging ports and faulty chargers.

Cracked or faulty screens

Cracked, flickering, dim, or blank screens. We source the right replacement screen for your model and quote before we start.

Overheating and noisy fans

Laptops that run hot, shut down by themselves, or sound like a jet engine. A clean-out and service usually brings them back to normal.

Keyboards, trackpads, and hinges

Sticky or missing keys, trackpads that will not respond, and hinges that are loose or broken.

Spills and drops

Coffee on the keyboard or a fall off the desk? Turn the laptop off, do not charge it, and get in touch as soon as you can.

Slow or ageing laptops

A faster SSD and more memory can make an older laptop feel new again. For software problems, see Windows support.

Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Can you replace my laptop battery?

For most models, yes. We confirm the right part and cost with you before any work starts.

Can you fix a cracked laptop screen?

In most cases. We check your exact model, source a matching screen, and give you a quote first.

Why is my laptop overheating?

Usually dust build-up inside or dried-out thermal paste. A clean and service normally fixes it and helps your laptop last longer.

I spilled something on my laptop. What should I do?

Turn it off straight away, unplug it, and do not try to switch it back on. Then call us on 03 6449 4214. The sooner we see it, the better the chance of saving it.

Is my old laptop worth repairing?

We will tell you honestly. If a repair costs more than the laptop is worth, we will say so and help you choose a replacement.

How long do repairs take?

It depends on the problem and parts, but we focus on fast turnaround and keep you updated the whole way.
