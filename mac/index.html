What we fix

Mac Problems We Fix

Part of our computer repairs service. We support MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac and Mac mini.

Slow Performance

Spinning wheels, slow start-ups and apps that take forever to open. We find what’s slowing your Mac down and fix it.

macOS Updates

Updates that won’t install or a Mac stuck on an old version. We check what your Mac can run and update it safely.

Storage Full

“Your startup disk is almost full” messages. We clear out what you don’t need and set up storage that works for you.

Apple ID & iCloud

iCloud setup, syncing photos and files between your Mac and iPhone, and Mail, Contacts and Calendar setup.

Moving From a PC to a Mac

New to Mac? We move your files, photos and email across from your old Windows PC and show you around.

MacBook Battery, Screen & Keyboard Issues

Battery draining fast, a damaged screen or keys that have stopped working? We’ll diagnose the problem and talk you through your repair options.

Backups, Pop-ups & Scams

Time Machine backups, plus help with suspicious pop-ups, adware and scams. If you’ve been caught out, see scam recovery.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Mac support

Which Macs do you work on?

MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac and Mac mini.

My Mac says the startup disk is full. Can you help?

Yes. We work out what’s taking up the space, safely clear what you don’t need and set up iCloud or external storage if it helps.

Can you help me move from a Windows PC to a Mac?

Yes. We transfer your files, photos and email and help you get comfortable with your new Mac.

Can Macs get viruses?

It’s less common than on Windows, but Macs can pick up adware, scam pop-ups and malware. We can check and clean your Mac.

Can my Mac run the latest macOS?

We check your model and let you know which version it can safely run.

Can you set up Time Machine backups?

Yes. We set up automatic backups so your files are protected if anything goes wrong.
