Managed IT

Support that removes repeat IT friction.

Managed IT support should do more than wait for something to break. We help Tasmanian teams reduce recurring issues, standardise devices, manage accounts properly, and keep the everyday technology stack stable enough that people can focus on work.

That can include helpdesk, monitoring, procurement advice, Microsoft 365 administration, endpoint protection, Splashtop remote access, backup checks, network support, and sensible documentation so your business is not reliant on one person remembering how everything works.

What good looks like

  • Staff know where to ask for help and what happens next.
  • Devices, accounts, and access are easier to onboard and offboard.
  • Security, backup, and support are built into daily operations.
Stack fit

Built around technology we can support confidently.

A good managed environment is consistent, documented, secure, and easy to support. We prefer practical systems that fit small and medium Tasmanian businesses rather than fragile one-off setups.

Common systems

  • Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Outlook
  • 1Password for shared password management
  • Splashtop for remote support where appropriate
  • Synology NAS and cloud backup planning
  • UniFi networking hardware for business Wi-Fi and switching

Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Do you only support businesses on managed agreements?

No. We can help with one-off support, projects, and managed arrangements. Managed support is best when a business wants ongoing helpdesk, monitoring, security, and continuity.

Can you support remote teams outside the North West?

Yes. We are North West based, but many support tasks can be handled remotely across Tasmania with onsite work planned where it makes sense.

Can you work with our existing Microsoft 365 tenant?

Yes. We can review the current setup, improve account governance, strengthen security, and support Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Outlook.

Ready to make IT support predictable?

Tell us how your team works now and where support is getting messy. We will help map the simplest managed path forward.
