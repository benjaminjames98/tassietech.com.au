Minimum Hardware Requirements

For TassieTech Managed Service Agreements, supported devices should be fit for purpose, owned by the company, and capable of running current secure software.

Computers

  • Less than 5 years old.
  • Windows 10 or newer, genuine licence.
  • macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
  • 2.4 GHz processor or better.
  • Minimum 4GB RAM, 8GB recommended.
  • 256GB SSD or faster storage.

Servers

  • Windows Server 2012 or newer.
  • Ubuntu 20.04 or newer.
  • Display support for 1280 x 1024 or higher.

Network and software

  • Networking equipment less than 5 years old.
  • Microsoft 365 licences or Microsoft Office 2022 or newer.
  • All devices must be appropriate for business use.
