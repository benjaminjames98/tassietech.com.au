What we fix

PC Problems We Fix

Part of our computer repairs service. We repair and upgrade desktop towers and all-in-one PCs from every major brand. Laptop trouble? See laptop repairs.

PCs That Won’t Turn On

No power, no display, beeping or a fan that spins with nothing on screen. We track down the faulty part, whether it’s the power supply, memory or motherboard.

Speed-Up Upgrades

Swapping an old hard drive for an SSD and adding memory is often the cheapest way to make a slow desktop feel new again.

Overheating & Noisy Towers

Dust-clogged fans and heatsinks cause loud fans, random shutdowns and crashes. A clean-out and service fixes most of them.

Failing Hard Drives

Clicking noises, files that won’t open or a desktop stuck on start-up can mean a drive is failing. We back up what we can and replace the drive.

New Desktop Advice & Setup

Not sure whether to repair or replace? We’ll give you honest advice, help you choose a new PC and set it up with your files moved across. For software and update issues, see Windows support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common desktop repair questions

My PC won’t turn on. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Most of the time it’s a single faulty part, like the power supply, and we’ll quote before replacing anything.

Is it worth upgrading my old PC?

Often. An SSD and more memory can make a big difference for much less than a new computer. We’ll tell you honestly if it’s not worth it.

Why is my desktop so loud?

Dust build-up makes the fans work harder. A professional clean usually makes it quieter and cooler and helps it last longer.

Can you get my files off a PC that won’t start?

In many cases we can recover files from the drive of a computer that won’t boot, and move them to your repaired or new PC.

Can you help me choose a new PC?

Yes. We’ll recommend a desktop that suits how you use it, then set it up and transfer your files.

Do you fix all-in-one PCs?

Yes, along with standard desktop towers from all the major brands.
