Recommended Technology Platform

The following tools represent a practical, supportable baseline for managed devices and common business workflows.

  • Windows 10 or newer, Windows Server 2012 or newer, Ubuntu 20.04 or newer, macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
  • Microsoft Office, LibreOffice, Outlook, Thunderbird, Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, and Mozilla Firefox.
  • 1Password, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Zoom, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet.
  • VirtualBox, OpenVPN, 7-Zip, Splashtop Streamer, and Splashtop Anti-Virus where appropriate.
