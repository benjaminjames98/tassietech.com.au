UniFi networks

Networks should be visible, documented, and boring in the best way.

A good network lets staff work, guests connect safely, devices stay in the right place, and support issues become easier to diagnose. We help businesses move from consumer-grade uncertainty to a cleaner UniFi network design where appropriate.

That can include Wi-Fi coverage, switch planning, firewall rules, VLANs, guest networks, IoT separation, remote visibility, documentation, firmware updates, and troubleshooting slow or unreliable connections.

What good looks like

  • Staff and guests connect to the right network.
  • Problem devices are easier to find and isolate.
  • The network is documented enough to support confidently.
Stack fit

Built around technology we can support confidently.

UniFi is useful when configured properly. The value comes from design, segmentation, naming, updates, and visibility rather than the hardware alone.

Common systems

  • UniFi gateways, switches, access points, and controllers
  • Guest Wi-Fi and segmented staff or device networks
  • Firewall rules and practical VLAN planning
  • Microsoft 365 and VoIP-aware network design
  • Backup connectivity and continuity planning where needed

Questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Do you only support UniFi networks?

No. We support a range of business networks, but UniFi is part of our preferred stack when it fits the site and budget.

Can you separate guest Wi-Fi from business systems?

Yes. We can design guest networks, staff networks, and device networks so access is cleaner and safer.

Can you troubleshoot poor Wi-Fi coverage?

Yes. We can review placement, signal issues, interference, roaming, and whether the network design matches the building.

Need your network cleaned up?

Send us the site size, current equipment, and what is unreliable. We will help plan the next practical step.
